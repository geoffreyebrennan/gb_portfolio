Detailed case studies

Roles and permissions overhaul

Role UX Team Lead → Product Manager
Company Curve Dental
Focus Access control & user management
Outcome 9-month path to GA

I led the redesign of Curve Dental's legacy roles-and-permissions system, which was blocking sales deals and exposing the business to security and fraud risks. Working across research, UX, architecture and rollout, I built a more granular, practice-appropriate model for custom roles, multi-site visibility and user management.

The release moved the product from an outdated, rigid setup to a centralised administration model that scaled from small practices to large DSOs. It also showed how a careful rollout plan, documentation and validation discipline matter as much as the UI itself.

Perio charting rebuild

Role UX Team Lead / Lead Designer
Company Curve Dental
Focus Clinical workflow redesign
Outcome 10× usage increase

Perio charting had been built in Flash, was technically obsolete, and so frustrating to use that many hygienists defaulted to paper records. I led the redesign from research through to general availability, uncovering the real variation in how clinicians examined patients and building a system that supported multiple workflows instead of forcing one standard.

We built an SVG-based graphical model, a tabular view, keyboard-first interactions and configurable exam flows. The result was a production tool clinicians actually used and one that became a major differentiator in the product.

Billing system update

Role Lead Designer / Product Manager
Company Curve Dental
Focus Billing workflow refinement
Outcome Faster, clearer and more scalable billing

The billing module overhaul focused on preserving the familiar logic of the legacy experience while making the most important actions more visible and less error-prone. I shaped the redesign around task isolation, smarter defaults, clearer filtering and a cleaner ledger experience for busy practice teams.

By improving visibility, payment and invoice flows, and the underlying structure of the module, the rebuild made the product easier to use while creating a stronger base for future feature work.
